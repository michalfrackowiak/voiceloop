## 2. AI Act Article 50 transparency questions for counsel (P3-11.1)

Reference use case: a hotel-booking voice agent where a guest speaks, STT produces a hypothesis, a text parser fills a booking form, and a booking record is created (`context.md` §1).

- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: does an AI Act Article 50 transparency duty apply to this voice agent in the jurisdictions where it is deployed, given that the served languages and jurisdictions are still open (`context.md` §16, question 3)?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: is the voice agent an AI system interacting directly with natural persons for Article 50 purposes, and which entity is treated as provider versus deployer for our Pipecat-based or direct-`log_turn` integration?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: what notice to callers that they are interacting with an AI system is appropriate for a hotel-booking call? Should it be given at call connect, before the first guest utterance, on every call, or at another point? Control to verify alongside the question: where is the current call script and its version stored?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: in which channel(s) should that notice be delivered (spoken prompt, IVR message, SMS or in-app text), and in which languages, given the served languages are currently open?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: how should accessibility for guests with hearing or speech impairments be handled (for example, a text alternative or relay/TTY compatibility), and what does counsel consider adequate under Article 50 and any applicable accessibility rules?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: is a **recording notice** distinct from any service-quality or call-recording notice needed where audio is retained for future model training? If so, when and how should it be given? Control to verify: `consent.recording` is captured per call (`context.md` §12), but the legal sufficiency of any notice is OPEN.
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: when a call transfers to a human agent, how should the change from AI system to human be signalled, if at all?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: do TTS-synthesised variants used for training (`P3-03.1`) or any synthetic voice used in the call flow carry transparency or labelling duties, and who owns that determination?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: what evidence about notice should be retained for human review (for example, call-flow script version, notice text, and a timestamp in call logs), and for how long? See the retention matrix in section 5.

- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: if counsel identifies consent as a candidate basis, how specific must that consent be? Control to verify alongside the question: lineage from training example to source call and the deletion/impact report from `P2-12.2`.
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: is reuse of a recording made for service delivery or booking purposes compatible with the original purpose when it is used for model training (purpose limitation), and is any further assessment needed?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: what is the minimum necessary data for each purpose (data minimisation)? Must full-call audio be retained, or only failure slices; are the optional `contact.email` and `contact.phone` fields needed for training; is the whole `outcome.final_record` needed? - **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: which fields count as identifiers or pseudonymous identifiers in this pipeline (`call_id`, `guest_name`, `contact.email`, `contact.phone`, dates, room type), and is pseudonymisation before training (`context.md` §12) sufficient for counsel's purposes given adapters cannot be unlearned? - **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: is a data protection impact assessment (DPIA) required for recording calls, human review, and model training, and who owns and signs it?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: which third parties are processors (object store, Postgres hosting, MLflow hosting, the self-hosted Label Studio Community or Argilla instance, telemetry providers, CI and training infrastructure), and what processor agreements and sub-processor approvals are needed?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: where will audio, metadata, and artifacts be stored and processed, and are there international transfers outside the EEA or UK? If so, which transfer mechanism is needed? Control to verify: configured storage regions and vendor data-location terms.
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: does the EU guest scope in `context.md` §12 apply to this deployment at all, given `context.md` §16 question 3 is open? All EU-specific items above remain OPEN until a human answers.
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: how is a data subject located across systems when lineage is keyed by `call_id` rather than a guest identifier, and what identity verification is required for erasure requests?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Open question: are children or other vulnerable guests in scope, and would that change the consent or notice design?
- **Status: OPEN — counsel review required.** Control to verify: structured error responses contain no raw guest audio or PII (`docs/contracts.md`, "Structured errors"). Open question for counsel: are access and audit logs that contain a `call_id` acceptable?
