Failure injections:

`test_stt_failure_injection.py` asserts fail-closed behaviour before any
promotion for:

- stale, non-synthetic, promotion-claiming, mismatched, incomplete, invalid and
  missing shadow evidence;
- multiple chained refusals in one labelled `PromotionRefusalLog`;
- valid shadow evidence paired with a regressing e2e report;
- empty/truncated output, silence hallucination and multilingual forgetting;
- checkpoint URI substitution;

label, and leaves aliases and the promotion audit untouched. No audio bytes,
