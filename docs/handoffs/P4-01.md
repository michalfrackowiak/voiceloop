  - `settings.py` — typed `VOICELOOP_CANARY_` settings with explicit, enforced bounds: `canary_fraction ∈ [0, 0.20]` (`MAX_CANARY_FRACTION`), `window_seconds ∈ (0, 3600]` (`MAX_WINDOW_SECONDS`), `max_calls_per_tick ≤ 128`, `max_pairs ≤ 10_000`, `min_paired_calls ≥ 1`.   - `split.py` — deterministic call-level traffic split: SHA-256(`salt \x00 call_id`) → bucket in `[0,1)`; bucket below the bounded fraction joins the canary arm, otherwise champion. Stable across ticks, no randomness or clock. Rejects blank IDs and raw 13–19 digit runs (unredacted phone/card shapes). Emits only pseudonymous `call_id`, arm and bucket.
  - `metrics.py` — paired, descriptive outcome metrics from final booking records: per-call and per-field e2e booking accuracy, paired deltas, wins/losses/ties, error rates and every exclusion count. Both sides join by `call_id` alone; raw fields become SHA-256 digests immediately via the P3-07 `field_digests` helper. No WER input or output, no CI, no threshold and no promotion flag. `detect_regression()` fails closed on missing canary prediction, missing same-call baseline, missing outcome, empty final record or identity mismatch; non-scorable statuses/too few pairs are `insufficient` and handled at the window boundary by the runner; a strictly negative paired delta is `outcome_metric_regression`.
  - `rollback.py` — bounded, idempotent, hash-chained one-step rollback. The only write is `set(@champion, previous_version)` from an injected P2-09 `PromotionRecord`, cross-checked against the P3-06 `ReadOnlyAliasStore.get` protocol. Refuses outside the configured window, unbound/stale/mismatched alias and source errors. One successful flip appends exactly one canonical-JSONL audit record; audit failure compensates with a restore and reports `audit_write_failed`. Repeat calls produce `idempotent` with zero writes. `InMemoryRollbackAudit` (plus `CollectingRollbackAudit` alias) and file-backed `RollbackAuditLog` supplied; all expected failures are typed results, never raised.
  - `runner.py` — background-only `CanaryRunner.tick()`: consumes at most `max_calls_per_tick` observations from an injected `EventSource`, joins outcomes by `call_id` through the P3-07 `OutcomeSource`, builds paired digest-only evidence, scores metrics and asks the rollback controller for the single flip when required. `FakeEventSource`, `FakeClock` (from P3-07), `CollectingCanaryAudit`, `StructlogCanaryAudit` and `run_fixture_rehearsal()` supplied; no sleep/network/GPU/model/registry client. Every report and event carries the synthetic label, `promotion_decision: not_evaluated`, `champion_claim: false`, `gate_edits: 0` and `thresholds_changed: false`.

Known gaps / follow-ups:
- All seams are deterministic in-memory fakes. No live traffic, network, GPU, model runtime, MLflow alias, booking connector or human review is involved; §2 invariants 2 and 3 are not satisfied by this rehearsal.
- Descriptive paired point deltas have no bootstrap CIs. A real promotion judgment still needs P3-08 real shadow evidence, the P2-09 gate and §14 human sign-off; this package never promotes and never claims a champion.
- `RollbackAuditLog` appends locally to JSONL; the compensating restore on an audit failure is not transactionally atomic with a real MLflow write. Hard-crash atomicity remains a lead-owned write-ahead-audit follow-up (P3-06 note).
- Calls with non-scorable statuses are excluded; if exclusions leave too few pairs, rollback waits until the configured window boundary. No scheduler/service loop ships here; callers own the background loop that calls `tick()`.
- No real traffic split integration: production must supply the `EventSource`, outcome join, same-call champion baseline and read-only registry adapters (documented in the README). Buckets are salt-stable, not randomised per request.
