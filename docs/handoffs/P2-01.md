Known gaps / follow-ups:
- Fixture outcomes exist only on final turns and all of them match their parser outputs. No fixture fires `final_record_mismatch`; the highest-weight signal is verified with a derived mutation of a clean fixture event, not by faking a fixture label. Documented in README and asserted by tests.
- `date_confusion` and `nights_misparsed` have high confidence, healthy decoder stats and schema-valid parses with no outcome yet. An additional hypothesis-vs-parser number check (weight 0.50, below the final-record weight) makes these detectable without labels. It compares the event's own STT text with its parser output; no fixture ground truth is used by the scorer.
- Greeting turns intentionally carry `schema_valid: false`, `logprob: -1.85` and empty parser output. Empty output is exempt from schema/logprob signals; exact silence is still caught by decoder/confidence signals. A real dropped-all-slots parse cannot be distinguished from a no-slot parse until a final record or repair behaviour arrives.
- No agent-turn evidence exists in the fixture context JSONLs. Agent re-ask detection is unit-tested with synthetic records; transfer detection is unit-tested via `outcome.status`. Guest correction phrases are deliberately narrow to avoid flagging verified slot updates such as "Make that a deluxe room" and "And three nights, not two".
- The offline teacher is disabled by default behind `TeacherModel.judge(...)` with `FakeTeacherModel`. Real batch teacher work is P4-04; no model, network or GPU dependency was introduced.
- Fixture WAVs are tone/noise/silence carriers, so signals come from recorded event metadata, not audio decoding. Scores are triage hints, never labels or golden-set evidence (context.md §2.2 and §2.5). Random slice, clustering, caps and blind items are P2-02.
- No conflict with `context.md` §2 invariants was found. Offline-only operation preserves §2.1 (never in the call path), and no consent/PII/golden-set/promotion rule was changed.
